# Third-party asset provenance

The bank files are trademarked brand artwork. This file records the collection licences for
the repositories used during the 2026 audit; it does not grant permission to use any bank mark
in a commercial product.

## Indonesian vectors — `idn-finlogos` 2.5.0

- Repository: <https://github.com/hafidznoor/idn-finlogos>
- Asset source URL pattern: `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/idn-finlogos@2.5.0/dist/icons/`
- Collection licence: CC BY-NC 4.0, <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/legalcode>
- Notice: unofficial, community-maintained collection; the underlying trademarks remain with
  their respective owners.

## International vectors — `global-bank-logos`

- Repository: <https://github.com/auraveni/global-bank-logos>
- Asset source URL pattern: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/auraveni/global-bank-logos/main/`
- Collection licence: MIT, <https://opensource.org/license/mit>
- Notice: the repository's licence covers the collection, not the underlying trademarks.

## Additional vector — `simple-icons`

- Repository: <https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons>
- Asset source URL: recorded in `data/master_list.tsv` for the Monzo and Commerzbank rows.
- Collection licence: CC0 1.0, <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>
- Notice: trademark rights are not waived by the collection licence.

## Official raster artwork

KB Bank and KB Bank Syariah were imported from current/authoritative published sources and are
recorded per row in `data/master_list.tsv`. The KB Bank Syariah Commons record identifies the
bank's own site as the source: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:KB_Bank_Syariah.png>.

## Official-source vectors

The Siam Commercial Bank and ttb files are transparent SVGs based on current corporate artwork
published by the banks or their authoritative records, with the source URLs recorded per row in
`data/master_list.tsv`: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3ASiam_Commercial_Bank_Logo.svg>
and <https://www.ttbbank.com/th/about-us/ttb-logo>.
